How a round works.
  • Everyone acts at the same time — no turn order. The enemies move once every player has ended.
  • 3 Solar and 4 cards a round. Neither carries over.
  • Your hand is discarded at end of round; when the draw pile empties, the discard shuffles back in.
  • Shield is a stance, not a bank — it clears at the start of your next round.
  • Three fights, then the Extractor. Win one and everyone picks a card and patches up.
